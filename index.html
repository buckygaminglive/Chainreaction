How to Play

  • Tap any empty cell, or a cell you already own, to add an orb.
  • When a cell reaches its critical mass — 2 orbs in a corner, 3 on an edge, 4 in the middle — it explodes.
  • Exploding orbs shoot into neighboring cells, capturing them for you and possibly triggering more explosions.
  • Capture every orb on the board to eliminate opponents. Last player standing wins!
