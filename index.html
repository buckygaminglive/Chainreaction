How to play

  1. Charge a cell. Tap any empty cell, or one you already hold, to add a particle.
  2. Reach critical mass. A cell goes critical at 2 particles in a corner, 3 on an edge, 4 in the interior. Cells one particle short pulse — that's your warning.
  3. Propagate. A critical cell ejects one particle into each neighbour, converting them to your emission and possibly driving them critical too. That's the chain.
  4. Capture the field. Strip an opponent of every particle to eliminate them. Last emission standing wins.
