Take the whole board

Every particle on the board belongs to somebody. Knock out everyone else and the last colour standing wins.

One red cell left — one move from over.

Tap to add a particle

On your turn, tap an empty cell or one you already hold. That is the whole move. You can never add to a cell someone else owns.

Turns alternate — one particle each, no passing.

Every cell has a limit

A cell bursts the moment it reaches 2 particles in a corner, 3 along an edge, or 4 in the middle — one for each neighbour it touches.

Corners fill fastest. A cell one particle short pulses — that is your warning.

Full cells burst outward

When a cell hits its number it empties, firing one particle into each neighbour — and every cell it lands in becomes yours, whoever held it a moment ago.

That is the whole game. Territory changes hands by being hit, never by being outnumbered.

One burst sets off the next

The particles you fire can push those neighbours over their limits, and those over theirs. A single well-placed tap can roll across the whole board.

Chains win games. Set them up before you set them off.
