# CHAIN REACTION — Game Product Roadmap

**From Web Prototype to Premium Multiplayer Game, Google Play, iOS and YouTube Playables**

**Product Strategy • Game Design • Technology • Monetization • Platform Roadmap • Naming**

---

## 1. Product Vision

Build a polished, original, scientific-feeling multiplayer orb-reaction strategy game inspired by the classic chain-reaction genre, but differentiated through presentation, game modes, progression, online multiplayer, and its own visual identity.

The long-term product should work as a web game during development, then expand to Android/iOS and a streamlined YouTube Playable.

## 2. Current Prototype Baseline

Current web prototype: https://chainreaction-sooty.vercel.app/

Existing foundation includes the core chain-reaction mechanic, local play, online rooms with room codes, board-size options, orb customization and player colors.

Next priority is not feature quantity. The prototype should first become responsive, polished, satisfying and reliable.

## 3. Product Pillars

- **Instantly understandable:** a player should understand the objective within seconds.
- **Deep enough to master:** positioning, timing, chain planning and risk/reward should matter.
- **Social first:** private rooms, quick matches, rematches, rankings and shareable results.
- **Scientific identity:** clean, precise, laboratory-inspired visuals rather than fantasy or cartoon styling.
- **Premium game feel:** animation, sound, haptics, transitions, feedback and responsive UI should make every move feel meaningful.

## 4. Core Game

- Classic mode remains the foundation.
- Support 2–4 players.
- Board sizes: Small, Medium and Large.
- Local pass-and-play.
- Online private rooms.
- Later: public matchmaking, reconnection, rematch and spectator support.
- Server-authoritative online state should determine turns, legal moves, explosions, captures and winners.

## 5. New Game Modes

- **Classic** — the pure strategic experience.
- **Blitz** — short turn timers and faster matches.
- **Chaos** — controlled random events or special cells.
- **Survival** — progressively harder board conditions.
- **Team Battle** — 2v2 cooperative competition.
- **Power Cells** — carefully balanced special cells with unique effects.
- **Tournament** — best-of-3 or best-of-5 competitive brackets.

## 6. Game Feel & Presentation

- Premium particle effects for placement, charging, explosion and chain propagation.
- Clear anticipation before a cell reaches critical state.
- Impactful but readable explosions.
- Combo/chain counters and subtle screen feedback.
- Mobile haptic feedback for placement and major chain events.
- High-quality sound effects and restrained background music.
- Fast, smooth transitions with no unnecessary loading.

## 7. Scientific Visual Direction

Use a visual language inspired by molecular systems, particle physics, cellular automata, experimental laboratories and energy propagation.

Orb/cell designs should feel like physical scientific objects rather than generic colorful balls.

Boards can use subtle grid structures, measurement-like markings, energy traces, controlled glow and material depth.

UI should be minimal, precise and technical: strong typography, clean spacing, restrained color systems and clear information hierarchy.

Avoid copying the visual identity, assets, sounds or branding of the original game.

## 8. Online Multiplayer Architecture

- **Client:** rendering, input, animation, UI and prediction/interpolation where useful.
- **Backend:** authentication, room management, matchmaking, game state, turn validation, reconnection and results.
- Server-authoritative rules are essential for competitive integrity.
- Persist player profile, match history, rating and progression only after the core multiplayer loop is reliable.
- Plan for disconnect recovery and safe rematches from the beginning.

## 9. Progression & Retention

- Player profiles with level, wins, losses, games played, best chain and other meaningful statistics.
- Competitive ranks such as Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond and Master can be introduced after matchmaking is stable.
- Seasonal leaderboards, friends rankings, achievements and daily challenges can increase retention.
- Cosmetics should focus on orb skins, board themes, particle/explosion effects, avatars, victory animations and sound packs.
- Keep competitive power separate from paid cosmetics so the core game remains skill-based.

## 10. Monetization Strategy

- Google Play/iOS version can eventually support carefully designed advertising and optional purchases where platform policies allow.
- Cosmetics are a natural monetization layer.
- Premium themes, seasonal cosmetics and optional bundles can be considered.
- YouTube Playables should be treated as a streamlined acquisition/discovery experience rather than a copy of the full monetized app.
- Do not design the business model around monetization features that YouTube Playables do not support.

## 11. YouTube Playables Strategy

- Design responsively from the beginning for multiple aspect ratios and touch/mouse input.
- Do not assume a fixed 16:9 desktop canvas.
- Prepare a dedicated Playable build that integrates the YouTube Playables SDK and passes the Playables testing/certification requirements.
- Keep the Playable fast to load, easy to understand and immediately playable.
- Use the Playable as a top-of-funnel product that can expose players to the larger game ecosystem.

## 12. Platform Roadmap

1. Web prototype and internal testing.
2. Polished web beta with reliable online multiplayer.
3. Android build and Google Play testing.
4. iOS build and App Store testing.
5. YouTube Playables adaptation, SDK integration, test suite and certification.
6. Public launch, live operations, seasons and ongoing content.

## 14. Quality Bar Before Launch

- Stable frame rate on mid-range mobile devices.
- Fast initial load.
- No game-breaking desynchronization in online matches.
- Reconnect after temporary network loss.
- Readable board state at every moment.
- Accessible touch targets and responsive layouts.
- Consistent audio/visual feedback.
- Crash/error monitoring.
- Analytics for session length, retention, match completion, disconnects and mode popularity.
- Clear privacy, terms and platform-compliant account/payment flows where applicable.

## 15. Naming Direction — Scientific + Chain Reaction Feel

Naming should communicate reaction, propagation, particles, cells, energy, kinetics or systems without sounding like a generic sci-fi shooter.

Prefer short, memorable names that can work as an app icon, URL, logo and spoken brand.

Before final selection, check trademark conflicts, app-store availability, domain availability and social handles.

Strong conceptual directions include:

- Reaction kinetics
- Cascade
- Propagation
- Criticality
- Particle interaction
- Cellular systems
- Chain dynamics

## 16. Scientific Name Concepts

### REACTON

Reaction + particle-like naming; concise and brandable.

### REACTANCE

A real technical/scientific term with a strong game-brand feel.

### CASCADEX

Evokes cascading reactions with a modern game-brand sound.

### PROPAGA

Inspired by propagation; technical and distinctive.

### CHAIN DYNAMICS

A direct scientific connection to the core mechanic.

### CASCADE THEORY

Scientific, strategic and premium-sounding.

### PARTICLE CASCADE

Directly communicates the game's visual system.

### CRITICAL MASS

A powerful scientific phrase with strong strategic implications.

### ORBITAL REACTION

Scientific and visually evocative, although longer.

### REACTION FIELD

Descriptive with a strong scientific identity.

## 17. Recommended Naming Shortlist

1. **REACTON** — best balance of scientific identity, memorability and game branding.
2. **REACTANCE** — strongest technical tone; excellent if trademark/app-store clearance is available.
3. **CASCADEX** — modern game-brand feel while preserving the scientific idea.
4. **PROPAGA** — distinctive and directly connected to propagation.
5. **CHAIN DYNAMICS** — strongest literal scientific connection, but less compact as a brand.

## 18. Brand Direction for the Chosen Name

- **Logo:** geometric symbol based on interacting cells/orbs and a propagation path.
- **Typography:** modern technical sans-serif, with precise spacing and minimal decoration.
- **Icon:** one central charged cell triggering a surrounding cascade.
- **Palette:** dark graphite/near-black base with one or two controlled energy accents; avoid excessive rainbow effects.
- **Motion language:** pulses, propagation waves, measured expansion, energy transfer and clean particle trails.
- **Overall feeling:** laboratory-grade precision combined with competitive game intensity.

## 19. What Not To Do

- Do not simply clone the original game's branding or presentation.
- Do not add dozens of modes before the classic game feels excellent.
- Do not rely entirely on client-side validation for online competitive games.
- Do not lock the game to one screen ratio.
- Do not build monetization before retention and game feel are proven.
- Do not choose the final name before checking trademarks, app stores, domains and social handles.

## 20. Immediate Next Steps

1. Freeze the current prototype as a baseline build.
2. Define the final rules for Classic mode.
3. Choose the visual/scientific art direction.
4. Redesign the home screen, game HUD and result screen.
5. Improve chain animation, particles, sound and haptics.
6. Audit the online architecture and synchronization.
7. Create a mobile-first responsive layout system.
8. Decide the final brand name after a naming/legal availability check.
9. Build a polished beta.
10. Only then begin the platform-specific Google Play, iOS and YouTube Playables work.

# Core Product Philosophy

The target is **not simply another Chain Reaction clone**.

The target is:

> **A polished, original multiplayer orb strategy game with a scientific identity, satisfying chain-reaction gameplay, competitive depth, strong social features, and a platform strategy that can scale from web to mobile and YouTube Playables.**

The strongest long-term positioning is:

**Scientific + Competitive + Social + Satisfying + Premium.**
