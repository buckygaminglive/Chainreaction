REACTON is a browser game. It is designed to need as little about you as possible: there is no sign-up, no email address, no password, and no advertising or third-party tracking of any kind.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a small amount of data in your browser's own storage. It never leaves your device unless a section below says otherwise.

  • Your settings — game mode, board size, player count, particle style, volume, haptics and motion preferences.
  • Your local record — matches played, wins, best chain and best capture, used for the level shown on the title screen.
  • Your guest account — an identifier and a secret, if you have used the online screen (see below).
  • A seat token — held for the current browser tab only, so that a dropped connection can put you back in the same seat.

Clearing your browser's storage for this site erases all of it, including your guest account. There is currently no way to recover an account that has been cleared, and no way to move one to another device.

Guest accounts and online play

The first time you open the online screen, the game's relay server creates a guest account: a random identifier, a secret held only by your browser and the server, and a short friend code. There is no email address, no password and no profile.

If you play online, the relay holds:

  • the display name you choose, which other players in your room and anyone viewing a leaderboard can see;
  • your friend code, and the list of people you have added as friends;
  • your competitive record — rating, wins, losses, matches played and best chain;
  • the state of a room you are in while the match is running.

Choose a display name you are comfortable with strangers seeing. It appears on public leaderboards.

Technical logs

The relay runs on Cloudflare Workers. Like any web service, incoming requests carry an IP address, which is used to apply rate limits and may appear in the hosting provider's operational logs. The game does not store IP addresses itself, and does not associate them with your guest account.

Cloudflare processes this data as our hosting provider. Their privacy documentation is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Diagnostics

The game can report anonymous diagnostics — how long a session lasted, which modes are played, whether matches are finished or abandoned, how often connections drop, and technical errors. These are counts, durations and fixed labels. They never include your display name, room codes, account secrets or board positions.

Diagnostics are sent only when a collection endpoint is configured for the build you are using. You can switch them off at any time under Settings → Diagnostics, and the game honours your browser's "Do Not Track" setting automatically.

Cookies

The game sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage, described above, for settings and your account — not for tracking, and not shared with anyone.

Children

REACTON is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because there is no sign-up, we do not ask for an age. Your choices

  • Play entirely locally. Pass-and-play and the single-player modes create no account and contact no server.
  • Turn diagnostics off in Settings.
  • Delete everything by clearing site data in your browser.
  • Ask for server-side deletion of a guest account and its record by writing to [CONTACT EMAIL] with your friend code.
